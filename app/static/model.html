How this model was selected

From 11 candidates trained on identical splits, by the published weighted score — never by accuracy alone.

Final = 0.40·F1 + 0.25·ROC-AUC + 0.15·Accuracy + 0.10·Speed + 0.10·Efficiency · ties on performance go to the smaller, faster model · full comparison
